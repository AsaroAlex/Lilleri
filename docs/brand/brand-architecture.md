# Brand architecture

**DECISION — 2026-10-02.** One master brand **Lilleri**. Plans describe usefulness and permissions, never prestige. Aligned with brand-strategy D7 and the product/business review; prices below are commercial **HYPOTHESES**, not approved or live offers.

| Stage | Product-facing name | Hypothesis price | Allowed promise | Entry gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Launch | **Lilleri Gratis**; price-card label “Gratis” | €0 | The implemented core loop within published connection/history limits; privacy/security/export/deletion remain accessible | Free scope and coverage are truthful; no “unlimited” claim until costs/capabilities support it |
| Launch | **Lilleri Plus**; card “Plus” | €4,99/month or €39,99/year | More useful depth, history/customisation/analysis as actually shipped | Real entitlements, recurring costs, VAT/store economics and willingness-to-pay testing |
| Later | **Lilleri Famiglia** | €9,99/month or €79,99/year | Consent-aware shared household view with separate identities and member permissions | Sharing is implemented, audited and user-tested; no placeholder family billing before this |
| Reserved | **Lilleri Pro** | UNKNOWN | A future professional/personal separation workflow only if research justifies it | Separate product/economic/compliance decision; never a second consumer paid depth tier |
| Not planned | **Lilleri Business** | None | None | B2B is not the consumer MVP; require new evidence before proposing |

At launch the billing ladder is Gratis → Plus. Earlier Free/Plus/Pro/Family economic scenarios are sensitivity comparators, not permission to ship four prices. “Lilleri” alone remains the app name regardless of plan; the store icon does not change by tier. The developer scope is `@lilleri/*`; never expose implementation package names as consumer sub-brands.

## Proposal, critique, counterproposal, decision

Proposal: four named tiers to segment depth and household work. Critique: Plus/Pro descriptions overlap, add a buying decision before value is proven, and a family SKU without sharing would sell a label. Counterproposal: one consumer paid tier and a separately gated household tier. Decision: launch Gratis/Plus only; Famiglia later, Pro reserved for professional context. User-rule precedence and correctness never become a premium “accuracy mode”. Privacy cannot be the upsell.

## Name and lockup rules

Use the same outlined wordmark in every tier. Plan descriptor is ordinary Geist 600 text adjacent to the wordmark with≥one stem clear space and baseline alignment; it is not fused into a new logo. Do not modify the li dot to a plus, gold badge, two-person glyph or crown. In prose write “Lilleri Plus”; inside a labelled plan selector “Plus” is sufficient. The formal free plan is Lilleri Gratis, while app name remains Lilleri. No Premium/Gold/Metal/Ultra/VIP/Elite and no “Lilleri+”.

Italy: Famiglia. English: Family; French: Famille; German: Familie; Spanish: Familia. Plus travels unchanged, translated descriptors in supporting copy explain it. Pro stays reserved; any localisation must be reviewed for professional/regulatory connotations. Localisation is a planned policy, not a claim of current market support or trademark availability.

## Commercial voice and trust

Show total annual price as the primary annual offer, billed period, monthly alternative, taxes according to verified local rules, cancellation/manage subscription route, and real entitlement differences. Do not gate reconnection correctness, undo, explanation, export, deletion or baseline security as marketing pressure. No countdown discounts, preselected annual hiding total price, forced trial card collection or auto-renew surprise.

HYPOTHESIS: a30-day **non-renewing Plus preview**, no payment details; afterwards return to Gratis unless the person actively subscribes. Current synthetic beta/demo is free. These are not store introductory auto-renew offers or promises that billing is implemented. Any paid offer is added only when cancellation/receipt/entitlement paths work.

Business review permits possible optional labelled partner offers later. Avoid “Niente pubblicità. Mai.” across marketing if that rail remains under consideration. Draft policy line: “Niente banner. Non vendiamo i tuoi dati.” Optional offers require a dedicated legal/trust decision and separate clear choice; no sponsored content in review actions or financial explanations. Privacy controls stay available on every plan.
