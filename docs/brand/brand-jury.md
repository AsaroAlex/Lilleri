# Brand jury and consolidation decision

**DECISION — 2026-10-02:** Direction **B, the li monogram**, with **T3 Carta & Vinaccia** and Geist UI typography. The existing A/B/C and T1/T2/T3 explorations were preserved. Four disciplinary lenses below are structured expert assessments by the implementation team, **not four recruited people, an investor endorsement or a consumer research panel**. Scores are subjective hypotheses on a 1–10 scale. No recognition times or statistical confidence are claimed.

## Proposal → critique → counterproposal → decision

Proposal: B's li monogram in its original wine/cool-night system. Critique: li is not an exclusive shape; tight foot gap fails small-format separation; cool night makes the warm-paper story incoherent; signature wine may be read as loss. Counterproposal: A's animated setting tile, or C's olive object, or B in warm neutrals with size-specific engineering. Decision: B retained; shortened foot and enlarged 832-unit viewport for 16/24px favicon, warm T3 night, semantic teal/orange with labels, ordinary debits in ink. A contributes one settling motion; C contributes material restraint and calm surfaces. No loser symbol is pasted into B.

## Red-team answers

| Question | Expert conclusion and practical limit |
|---|---|
| Crypto / gaming? | Flat paper, no neon, token/coin/rising-chart motifs or FX animation; unlikely by design, user association unmeasured |
| Name unserious / only Gen Z? | Lowercase alone is not childish; adult type, precise amounts and restrained Tuscan story reduce risk. Test ages 25–65, non-Tuscan regions and low financial confidence |
| Looks like a bank? | No shield, institutional blue or status-metal tier. Read-only mechanism must carry trust; graphic styling cannot substitute for it |
| Dated in two years? | Simple paths and neutral editorial system support longevity; this is a design hypothesis, not a forecast |
| Distinguishable from Revolut/N26/Klarna/Satispay/Wise/Curve and PFMs? | Warm paper/wine and li rhythm differ from the sampled public assets. Detailed style/word-name clearance and a licensed side-by-side study are open; generic shelves do not prove competitor discrimination |
| Palette distinctive? | Distinct combination in sampled research; wine alone is not exclusive. Keep one signature and strong neutral proportions |
| Shelf works? | At60px the tall-short rhythm/dot remains visible in seeds3/11; paper is quieter than saturated stand-ins. Does not establish measured recognition or confusion rate |
| `€12.348,92` legible? | Actual Geist outlined specimens show separators and euro at 14/20/32px; tabular digits each0.600em. Device and user reading checks remain |
| Outside Italy / millions of users? | No currency/flag in symbol; Lilleri spelling and Tuscan meaning require local naming/legal review. Design scales; commercial credibility requires reliable service, not a logo claim |

## Remaining release gates

Designer finalisation of all generated vectors and kerning; legal trademark/name/domain investigation (existing Lilleri/Lili/Lillero concerns); 20-person comprehension/confusion test using correctly licensed current competitor assets; accessibility tests on devices and assistive technology; App Store/Play current icon policies; release claims backed by shipped connector/engine behaviour. Gate G is **conceptually satisfied for implementation, not market-validated or legally cleared**.
