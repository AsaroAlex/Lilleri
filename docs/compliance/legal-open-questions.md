## 1. Executive summary

1. **Four questions block production**: (Q1) whether Banca d'Italia accepts an unlicensed B2C app receiving account data under a provider's AISP licence, and what the consent flow must say; (Q2) whether the category taxonomy is an Art. 9 inference and how to gate or avoid it; (Q3) the lawful-basis map including third-party-AI processing, vendor transfers and the explicit-permission step; (Q4) the provider contract itself (licensed EU entity and Italian passport, consent copy, DORA/outsourcing flow-downs, data roles, exit). All four are answerable in weeks with the right counsel and do not require a licence application.
2. **Five questions must close before public launch** (P1): AML applicability and alert wording (Q5); the AI-Act classification memo with L. 132/2025 and the minors policy (Q6); DORA/outsourcing contractual flow-downs and incident cooperation (Q7); consumer T&Cs, withdrawal, renewal and a dark-pattern audit (Q8); whether a DPO is mandatory (Q9).
3. **Feature-gated questions** (P2): affiliate/financial-promotion rules (Q10) — the business-model doc must not count on referral revenue until answered; cross-border expansion (Q11); e-mail ingestion and Android notification access (Q12–Q13); product-liability posture for AI insights (Q14); investment data and the MiFID boundary (Q15); teen accounts (Q16).
4. **Watch items** (P3): EAA classification (Q17); PSR agreed text (Q18); own-AISP registration prerequisites incl. AML and DORA (Q19).
5. **Suggested engagements**: one Italian fintech-regulatory firm (Q1, Q4, Q5, Q7, Q10, Q19, Q15), one privacy/GDPR counsel working with the external DPO (Q2, Q3, Q9, Q12, Q13, Q16), one consumer/advertising-law counsel (Q8, Q10 advertising half, Q14), one AI/technology counsel (Q6, Q14) — the second and fourth can be the same firm. DECISION (proposed).
6. **Biggest risk if nothing is asked**: launching under model A with a consent flow that does not name Lilleri and the purpose, a taxonomy that infers health or beliefs, and transaction text flowing to a US LLM without permission — three independent ways to be re-characterised, fined or removed from the stores in the first months. HYPOTHESIS grounded in RL-§6.2, §10.2, §11.1.
