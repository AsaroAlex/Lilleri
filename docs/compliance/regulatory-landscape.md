## 1. Executive summary (decision-relevant)

1. **The law in force for bank data is PSD2 + the RTS on SCA (Delegated Reg. 2018/389 as amended by 2022/2360), transposed in Italy by D.lgs. 218/2017.** It fixes three product parameters: SCA renewal at the bank at least every **180 days**, at most **4 unattended refreshes per 24 h** per consent, and typically **~90 days of history** at first connection. FACT (RL-§1.1, PA-§2.1, PB-§1).
2. **PSD3 and the PSR are not law.** Politically agreed 27 Nov 2025, compromise texts 23 Apr 2026, ECON 5 May 2026, but on 2026-10-02 both files are "awaiting Council's 1st reading position" with only an *indicative* plenary on 14 Dec 2026; no OJ number exists; PSR application is 18 months after entry into force → **mid-2028 at the earliest**. FACT (reported, convergent; RL-§2.1, PA-§2.2, PB-§1). Secondary descriptions of the agreed text say the 4×/day cap is **retained** and renewal SCA moves to the AISP; 180 vs 365 days is UNKNOWN.
3. **FIDA (open finance) is not agreed**; no provisional agreement reported as of 30 Sep 2026; application ≥2028–2029. FACT (reported; RL-§3). No build dependency; investments/insurance arrive by manual import until then.
4. **Lilleri can go live without a licence only as a data recipient under the provider's AISP licence (model A) or, exceptionally, as a registered agent (model B).** Model A is documented by Tink, TrueLayer, Yapily, Enable Banking, Mastercard, Powens and Fabrick; model B is legally foreseen but commercially exceptional (Tink AB has one agent in total). **Banca d'Italia's written position on model A was not found in any pass** → the single P0 legal question. FACT (providers) / UNKNOWN (supervisor) (RL-§10, PA-§2.6, PB-§15).
5. **DORA applies to the licensed provider, not to unlicensed Lilleri**; expect Art. 30 contractual flow-downs (audit, incident cooperation, data location, exit) and, since 22 Jul 2025, the subcontracting RTS (Delegated Reg. 2025/532) shaping those clauses. FACT (RL-§4; NEW-4).
6. **AI Act: PFM categorisation and insights are not Annex III high-risk** (only creditworthiness scoring is); **Art. 50 chatbot transparency applies since 2 Aug 2026 and was not deferred** by the Digital Omnibus on AI (Reg. (EU) 2026/1744, in force 27 Jul 2026, which deferred Annex III to 2 Dec 2027); Italian L. 132/2025 (in force 10 Oct 2025) adds plain-language disclosure and a minors rule. FACT (RL-§5, reported for the Omnibus).
7. **GDPR is the heaviest day-one burden**: contract as lawful basis for the core service, **Art. 9 inference risk** in the category taxonomy, DPIA mandatory, DPO strongly advisable, EU residency, SCC + TIA as fallback while the DPF appeal (C-703/25 P) is pending with no hearing date. FACT (RL-§6; NEW-5). Detailed in `privacy-model.md`.
8. **ePrivacy/Codice privacy art. 122 and 130** govern device identifiers, analytics SDKs and marketing push; the Digital Omnibus "data track" that would move cookie rules into the GDPR is **stalled** (Council mandate vote cancelled; Art. 88a/88b deleted from the June 2026 presidency compromise) — proposal only. FACT (reported; RL-§7; NEW-6).
9. **Store rules are the most concrete day-one gates**: Apple 5.1.2(i) (explicit permission before sharing personal data with third-party AI, verbatim since 13 Nov 2025), 3.2.1(viii) (money-management apps "should be submitted by the financial institution"), 5.1.1(v) (in-app account deletion), IAP at 15% under the Small Business Program (EU alternative terms effective 1 Oct 2026 save ≈2 points at best); Google Play **clarified on 15 Jul 2026 that its User Data policy applies to third-party AI integrations** (this closes the raw note's UNKNOWN), fees 10% service + 5% billing since 30 Jun 2026, Financial-features declaration, Data safety, account deletion. FACT (first-hand for Apple/Play fees; search-snippet for the July 2026 Play clarification — NEW-1).
10. **Monetised financial referrals are regulated in Italy** (OAM *mediazione creditizia* for loans/cards, IVASS RUI for insurance, MiFID advice for personalised investment recommendations; CCD2 transposed by D.lgs. 212/2025 in force 10 Jan 2026 tightens credit advertising). **v1 ships without monetised referrals.** FACT (RL-§8; NEW-2). AML: no obligations for an unlicensed recipient; NIS2: out of scope; EAA: micro-enterprise exemption but build WCAG 2.1 AA anyway.

### 4.3 AI Act + L. 132/2025 — obligations list (FACT unless stated)

1. **Art. 50(1)** (since 2 Aug 2026): tell the user they are interacting with an AI system unless obvious; L. 132/2025 adds plain-language information on how AI affects them. → Chat disclosure on first use and in settings; "Categoria suggerita dall'AI" labels.
2. **Art. 4** (softened): take measures to support AI literacy of staff → short internal training + policy, documented.
3. **Art. 5**: no manipulative/exploitative techniques → DECISION: no upgrade or offer prompt triggered by distress signals (overdraft, failed direct debit, low balance).
4. **Deployer duties / documentation**: AI system description ("information to the user only; not used for credit decisions"), model and vendor inventory, vendor AI-Act documentation on file (RL-§5.3). ASSUMPTION on the exact deployer checklist for a non-high-risk system — light counsel memo.
5. **Minors**: L. 132/2025 (<14 parental consent) + Codice privacy art. 2-quinquies (digital consent age 14) → DECISION: **18+ at launch**.
6. **Score discipline**: any "financial health" or affordability indicator stays user-facing, never shared with lenders or used to gate offers (ASSUMPTION that this keeps it outside Annex III 5(b); counsel to confirm before any such feature).
