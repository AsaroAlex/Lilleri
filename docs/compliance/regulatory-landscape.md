## How to read this document

- **FACT** means a claim supported at the stated evidence level; a provider's documented offer is a fact about that offer, not authorisation of Lilleri. **ASSUMPTION** means a working input; **HYPOTHESIS** an interpretation requiring validation; **DECISION** a project choice; **UNKNOWN / OPEN QUESTION** a gap.
- Evidence: `docs/research/raw/regulatory-landscape.md` (**RL-§x**, **RL S-nn**), `non-bank-sources-and-os-limits.md` (**NB-§x**), `open-banking-providers-a.md` (**PA-§x**, **PA #nn**) and `open-banking-providers-b.md` (**PB-§x**, **PB #nn**). **NEW-n** identifies sources reported by the earlier synthesis, retained in §8.2; it does not describe searches performed during this review.

Institutional legal hosts were inaccessible in the retained research; many 2026 status claims came from snippets or third-party repositories. Repetition across repositories does not establish independent corroboration. A primary URL's authority does not establish that its text was read. Current consolidated text and procedural status remain **UNKNOWN at production assurance level** until read by counsel/compliance.
- “In force”, “adopted”, and “applicable” are different states. A future application date is conditional on adoption and final provisions. Store rules are contractual policy, not legislation.

## 1. Executive summary

1. **DECISION:** use PSD2 and RTS 2018/389 as amended by 2022/2360 as the conservative engineering baseline from retained evidence (RL-§1.1). The reported 180-day AIS SCA exemption/renewal mechanics, default four unattended accesses per 24 hours and provider history windows are separate concepts. None promises a 180-day connection, four successful syncs or 90 days of obtainable history.
2. **FACT (provider documentation/mirrors and reported pages):** several vendors market AIS for unregulated customers (PA-§2.6, PB-§15). **UNKNOWN:** whether Lilleri's actual activities, contracting entity, Italian passport and approved UX fit a lawful recipient arrangement. The provider's licence does not automatically authorise Lilleri to provide AIS. Route A is a **HYPOTHESIS**, gated by written counsel opinion and provider terms.
3. **DECISION:** PSD3, PSR, FIDA and unverified 2026 amendments create no implementation dependency. Retained notes report PSD3/PSR agreement without adoption and FIDA without agreement; this review cannot freshly certify those negative status claims or infer a firm launch/application date.
4. **DECISION:** complete a DPIA, lawful-basis/Article 9 review, provider-role allocation, vendor/transfer review and tested deletion/incident procedures before any real-user financial-data beta. The likely DPIA requirement is a **HYPOTHESIS** based on processing risks; completing it is a project gate regardless of threshold.
5. **DECISION:** v1 excludes payment initiation, monetised financial referrals, lender-facing scores, mailbox OAuth, notification listening and teen accounts. Official read-only sources and explicit imports still require source-specific legal and contractual assessment.

## 4. Concrete product constraints (DECISION unless labelled)

| Constraint | Resolution |
|---|---|
| **Role separation** | Provider performs licensed AIS under its own scope; Lilleri's own conduct must fit the reviewed recipient arrangement. No “Lilleri is covered by [Provider]'s licence” claim. Registered agency requires its own documented process; neither is approved here. |
| **AIS consent / SCA / sessions** | Maintain separate purpose/account scope, consent validity, SCA-exemption/renewal requirement and token/session expiries. A fresh token or SCA does not itself establish valid consent or GDPR basis. |
| **180 days** | Retained description of an AIS SCA exemption/renewal interval, not universal consent validity. Earlier challenge may occur on justified fraud/security grounds or provider/bank limitations. Read actual status and renewal action. |
| **Four accesses** | Conservative default for unattended requests within relevant RTS scope; not four successful daily updates or a blanket unrestricted on-open refresh entitlement. Provider limits, bank scope, paging and any valid higher-access arrangement must be mapped. |
| **History** | Use provider/bank-returned availability; 90-day exemption/history descriptions are not a guarantee or legal maximum. Tink late-renewal behaviour (PA #43) is provider-specific. Identify gaps from observed coverage, not elapsed days alone. |
| **Sensitive data** | Financial data is highly confidential; PSD2 “sensitive payment data” and GDPR Articles 9/10 are different definitions. Never collect credentials/full PAN by project policy; raw descriptions may still reveal Article 9 data. |
| **AI and vendors** | Rules/in-house mode first; external personal-data AI calls disabled pending optional permission, purpose/basis, vendor-role, transfer and Article 9 review. Vendor location does not establish processing location. |
| **Accuracy and user control** | Source timestamps, explanations, reversible corrections; no instrument-specific advice, lender scores or distress-triggered offers. Export/delete/revoke are product requirements. |
| **Store disclosures** | Assess each actual flow against each store's definitions and processor exceptions. Do not pre-fill “not shared” or “no tracking” without an SDK/vendor audit. |
