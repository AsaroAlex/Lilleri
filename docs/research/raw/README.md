# Raw research notes

Working notes produced by specialist research agents on 2026-10-02.
These are inputs to the synthesized documents in `docs/research/`, `docs/compliance/`,
`docs/business/` and `docs/brand/`. Every claim carries a status label:

- **FACT** — verified against a cited source on the verification date.
- **ASSUMPTION** — working value used for planning, not verified.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — belief to be validated with users/data.
- **DECISION** — a choice made by the team, with rationale.
- **OPEN QUESTION / UNKNOWN** — could not be verified; includes how to verify.

Research environment constraint: web search was available, but direct page fetching
was blocked for most domains by the network egress policy. Claims are therefore based on
search-engine snippets and summaries and must be re-verified against the primary source
before any contractual, legal or pricing decision.
