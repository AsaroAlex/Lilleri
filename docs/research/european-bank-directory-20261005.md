## Entity and route decisions

Berliner Sparkasse is used instead of the nationwide Sparkasse umbrella. Bank
Austria is used instead of a regional Raiffeisen umbrella. Nordea Norway is used
instead of the SpareBank 1 alliance. Danske Bank Finland is used instead of the OP
cooperative-bank umbrella. La Banque Postale is used instead of the French
Crédit Agricole regional network. These choices produce concrete, recognizable
consumer website references; the eventual live provider still needs exact
institution and branch bindings where applicable.

Millennium's current consumer root is used instead of
`ind.millenniumbcp.pt/pt/particulares/Pages/default.aspx`, which returned a login
page. Nordea Finland uses `/en/`; the older `/en/personal.html` failed retrieval.
Germany's N26 page and Italy's existing `/it-it` reference describe separate
consumer markets. No additional countries are inferred for bunq, Monzo,
Starling, Revolut or N26 merely because their apps are international. Wise is
outside this bank-directory expansion; a payment or e-money service must not be
relabeled as a bank from its marketing name.

The production runtime's automatic routes remain `configuration_required`
unless protected identity, entitled live-provider discovery, an admitted consent
and callback route, exact trusted institution bindings and account-kind evidence
are supplied. An official website is not integration evidence. No CSV/XLSX/PDF
export capability is assigned to a new entry from these pages. All new statement
capabilities remain unverified until independently established for the consumer
service and account.
